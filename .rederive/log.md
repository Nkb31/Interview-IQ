## D1. Protecting user identity
struggled with: the user was not sure how a browser app could keep identity without exposing it to the page or to scripts
found on their own: a server-issued credential that the browser cannot read or edit, and a server-side check before every protected action
did well: they rejected the weak browser-storage option and wanted the identity to stay hidden from the browser
did ok: the idea needed a small nudge to clarify that the browser should not hold the trusted credential itself
better idea: none
